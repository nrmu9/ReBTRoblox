ReBTRoblox
========

ReBTRoblox does not collect any personal data. Nothing is sent to its
developers or to anyone else but Roblox, whose own sites it talks to as you.

- Your settings, the activity ReBTRoblox records, and its log of recent errors
  are kept in your browser, on your device.
- With Sync Settings on, your settings, including the people you watch and the
  games and communities you track, are also kept in your browser account's sync
  storage (Firefox Sync or Chrome sync), so they reach your other computers.
  Recorded activity is never synced. Turn it off under Backup in the settings.
- A bug report is only sent if you send it: Report on GitHub opens a GitHub
  form, and the error report is pasted by you.
